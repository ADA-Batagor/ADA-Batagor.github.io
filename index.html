Why Batagor

Phones fill up. We'd like to help them breathe.

Batagor is four of us: product, design and marketing, engineering, and a tech lead, building apps for the love of it.

It started with one itch. Our phones are full of things we never decided to keep. A Day was the first answer: a camera that forgets after 24 hours, so your camera roll doesn't have to hold every receipt and half-finished meme.

Batu is the second: a block of storage you set aside, so when you're out of room at exactly the wrong moment, there's a way out that takes one tap.

  1. Private by construction

    No accounts, no ads, no analytics in either app. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing about you for us to keep.

  2. One job each

    Every app does a single thing and gets out of the way. If a screen doesn't help with that, it doesn't ship.

  3. Plain, honest copy

    We say what an app does and nothing it doesn't. No guilt, no tricks, no pushing you toward anything.
